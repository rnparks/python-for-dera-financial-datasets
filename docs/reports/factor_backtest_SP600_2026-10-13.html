Do accounting factors pay in the SP600?

Point-in-time fundamentals from SEC DERA, month-end prices, equal-weight quintile sorts rebalanced monthly, quarterly and annually, measured raw and against the Fama-French five factors and momentum. Generated 2026-10-13 from the database by tools/factor_report.py.

Verdict: nothing pays beyond chance. 2 of 30 sorts clear t 2 after the five factors and momentum, which is what 30 tests produce from noise. Across the composite and nine factors at three rebalancing frequencies, 1 of 30 raw spreads clear t 2 (Gross profit / assets at annual rebalancing, -9.8% a year, t -2.03). After the five-factor model the profitability sorts are largely their factor loadings: 0.39 to 0.94 on profitability (RMW), -0.41 to 0.09 on value (HML), and 0.21 to 0.34 on momentum for the quarterly signals. 2 of 30 tests clear t 2 on FF5 (Operating margin at monthly rebalancing, -7.9% a year, t -2.48; Operating margin at quarterly rebalancing, -7.9% a year, t -2.32) and 2 on FF5 plus momentum (Operating margin at monthly rebalancing, -8.0% a year, t -2.53; Operating margin at quarterly rebalancing, -8.0% a year, t -2.36). With 30 tests, one or two |t| above 2 are what chance delivers at the 5% level.

The machinery checks out: the equal-weight benchmark of all members earns 9.5% a year with an FF5 alpha of -0.3%, market beta 0.98 and R² 0.98. The Yahoo returns and the French series line up.

4. Why the spreads are biased down, not up

  • Survivors only. Yahoo returns nothing for a delisted ticker, so 718 of the 2,417 companies ever in the S&P 1500 have no prices. Failures and takeovers leave the index from the bottom quintile more than the top; their missing returns flatter Q1 and shrink the measured spread. The share of panel members with a price the following month rises from 64.8% in 2018 to 93.4% by 2025.
  • Small caps. Where the published premia are largest, and where survivorship bias is largest too: small companies fail and get bought far more often, and every one that did is missing from these returns. Treat the bottom-bucket returns here with the most suspicion.
  • The first year is thinner. The composite covers 509 of 590 members in 2018 (the surprise factor needs eight quarters of history and DERA begins in 2009) and 569 or more a date from 2021.
  • 30 tests. Ten sorts on three schedules; at the 5% level one or two |t| > 2 are expected from noise alone.

6. Method, exactly

  • Universe. sec_reference.index_members('SP600', date): the constituents on each month end from the replayed index history, one row per company (a seat whose ticker the crosswalk still assigns to its previous holder cannot count a company twice; check 71).
  • Factors. sec_gold.factor_sheet_asof, stored per month end in sec_market.factor_panel. Annual factors come from the newest fiscal year filed by the date (not older than 550 days), quarterly ones from the newest quarter filed by the date (not older than 200 days). Every input is keyed on tradable_from, the first session a filing could be traded on, never the filing date; 57% of filings are accepted after the close. The panel's CHECK constraints refuse any row whose period end or availability is after its own knowledge date.
  • Suspect facts. 5,546 facts whose period ends after their own filing date and 87 balance-sheet lines printed in thousands or millions without saying so are refused by every gold table (sec_gold.suspect_fact), decided from the filing and earlier filings only.
  • Prices. Yahoo Finance month-end closes for the primary ticker of each surviving member (sec_market.monthly_price; 1864 of 1868 symbols answered on 2026-10-12). Returns use the dividend-and-split-adjusted close; a month is skipped across a gap of more than 35 days, when either month did not trade, or when the prior as-traded close was under a dollar.
  • Sort and hold. sec_market.backtest: on each rebalance date, NTILE(5) over the signed percentile among members that have the factor; equal weight within bucket; held through every month end up to and including the next rebalance; a member without a price that month drops out of its bucket for that month.
  • Statistics. sec_market.backtest_summary for means, t, Sharpe and hit rates; tools/ff_baseline.py for the regressions (Newey-West, six lags) on Ken French's monthly five factors and momentum, percent returns read as decimals.

7. What the build caught along the way

  • A company counted twice. The panel's primary key refused 2020-03-31: the S&P 500's IR and TT seats both resolved to Trane Technologies, because the ticker IR passed to Ingersoll Rand Inc on 2020-03-03 and the crosswalk moves a ticker at its next capture (2020-06-28). Three month ends, one company, the only such case since 2010; the sheet is now one row per company and check 71 pins the count.
  • A planner pathology. The gold layer is rebuilt in one transaction, so a freshly built matview has no statistics until commit. The peer-statistics build costed a correlated EXISTS as free and ran it as a per-row index scan for eleven hours before it was cancelled. The predicate is now a join, every matview is analysed as soon as it is indexed, and the rebuild takes 48 minutes.
  • Look-ahead by mis-dating. Catalent's 10-Q of 2024-02-14 dates a nine-month net income to 2024-03-31; the panel's CHECK caught it, and such facts are now refused at the source.
  • A scale error at the top of every sort. Hill-Rom's FY2021 10-K carries total assets of 4,999.1 dollars against 4,671.1 million for FY2020 in the same filing; it had produced a return on assets of 49,709.

8. Open

  • Delisted members have no prices; Polygon's flat files (or CRSP) are the source that closes the survivorship gap. Four open symbols Yahoo lacked: CUK, HCIIP, SATS, TDDWW.
  • S&P 400 and 600 panels (dera build-factor-panel --index SP400), where the published premia are larger.
  • Value-weighted buckets and a size control; the backtest is equal-weight only.
  • Ticker handoffs dated from the filings that announce them rather than the crosswalk's next capture.
